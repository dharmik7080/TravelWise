Features

Core Travel Toolkit

Equipped with state-of-the-art tools to build, manage, and predict your travel plans.

AI Recommendation

Our scoring algorithm evaluates states, season matches, and budgets to recommend your ideal getaway locations.

Budget Prediction (ML)

Utilizes linear regression machine learning models to forecast lodging, food, and transport costs based on travelers and packages.

Intelligent Itinerary Generator

Creates fully customized multi-day itineraries, distributing local sightseeing activities evenly using round-robin logic.

Live Weather API

Fetches real-time temperature, humidity, wind conditions, and visibility forecasts directly from open-source weather interfaces.

Smart Dashboard

Your command center to track upcoming trip statuses, review past journals, inspect spending analytics, and read AI suggestions.

Personal Wishlist

Bookmark destinations you wish to explore and save them to your profile with one-click toggles.
